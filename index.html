The Wealth Building Playbook

Each strategy is tested and proven to maximize your savings without sacrificing quality of life.

💳

The 24-Hour Rule

Wait 24 hours before any purchase over $50. 70% of impulse buys are regretted. This simple pause saves thousands annually.

💰 Save $2,000+/year
🏦

Automate Savings

Set up automatic transfers to savings on payday. What you don't see, you don't spend. Aim for 20% minimum — pay yourself first.

💰 Save $5,000+/year
📱

Subscription Audit

Cancel unused subscriptions monthly. Average person wastes $200/month on forgotten services. Review and cut ruthlessly.

💰 Save $2,400/year
🍳

Meal Prep Sundays

Batch cook for the week. Eating out costs 5x more than home cooking. One meal prep day saves 10+ hours and hundreds per week.

💰 Save $4,000+/year
⚡

Energy Vampire Hunt

Unplug devices when not in use. Standby power costs $100+ per year. Smart power strips automate this effortlessly.

💰 Save $150/year
🚗

Transportation Hack

Bike, walk, or carpool when possible. Gas + maintenance + insurance = $9,000/year average. Cut this in half with intention.

💰 Save $4,500/year
💧

Utility Negotiation

Call providers annually to negotiate rates. Most people overpay by 20%+ simply because they never ask. 10 minutes, real savings.

💰 Save $600/year
🛒

Generic Over Brand

Store brands are often identical to name brands (same factories). Pay for the product, not the marketing. Quality stays, price drops.

💰 Save $1,500/year
📊

50/30/20 Budget

50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. Simple framework that works. Track everything for one month — awareness changes behavior.

💰 System
🎯

No-Spend Challenges

Pick one category monthly to eliminate. No dining out January, no clothes February. Builds discipline and reveals true needs.

💰 Save $1,200/year
